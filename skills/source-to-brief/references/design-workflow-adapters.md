# Design Workflow Adapters

Prefer these adapters for Open Design, product, strategy, research, and artifact-preparation workflows.

## User Research Packet

Focus on:

- user groups
- jobs to be done
- pain points
- observed behaviors
- quotes
- unmet needs
- design opportunities
- risks and assumptions

## Interview / Transcript Set

Focus on:

- repeated patterns
- contradictions
- emotional language
- behavioral evidence
- workflow breakdowns
- needs
- open questions

## Competitive Scan

Focus on:

- category norms
- competitor promises
- feature patterns
- differentiation gaps
- pricing or positioning signals
- visual or interaction conventions
- opportunity spaces

## Market / Business Report

Focus on:

- market shifts
- segments
- demand signals
- value pools
- business constraints
- strategic implications
- artifact recommendations

## Policy / Compliance Source

Focus on:

- rules or constraints
- affected users
- required disclosures
- consent or data requirements
- design limitations
- risk areas

## Technical Source

Focus on:

- capability
- limitation
- implementation requirement
- latency, cost, or data constraints
- feasibility implication
- product risk

## Meeting Notes / Internal Memo

Focus on:

- decisions made
- unresolved questions
- stakeholder positions
- constraints
- next actions
- artifact implications
