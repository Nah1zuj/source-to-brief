# Evidence Confidence

Use confidence labels to prevent weak source material from becoming over-strong design direction.

## High Confidence

- repeated user interview patterns
- observed user behavior
- analytics data
- customer support logs
- verified internal metrics
- official policy or technical documentation
- direct source excerpts with stable location

## Medium Confidence

- small-sample interviews
- stakeholder statements
- credible industry reports
- competitor feature scans
- meeting notes
- sales or success anecdotes with partial detail

## Low Confidence

- vendor claims
- unsourced slides
- single anecdote
- speculative strategy memo
- model inference
- outdated market data
- search snippets

## Rule

Do not present low-confidence material as a strong design conclusion. Label it as an assumption, weak signal, or open question.
